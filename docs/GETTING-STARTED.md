# Getting started with GTM OS

Read this to the user in your own words on the first chat after install, then
offer the three choices at the end. Keep it short; they can ask for more.

---

Welcome to your outbound desk. Five apps are live and I am your SDR. Here is
what each one is, and what most teams change first.

**CRM** is the system of record: companies, contacts, deals. It opens with
sample records so you can see the shape. The first thing to change is the deal
stages. Tell me yours and I will rename them; the sample deals move with them.

**Prospector** is where lists come from. You describe who you want, I research
the live web and post named people with evidence, and the app buys their email
and phone through the finder keys you connect. It ships with no keys, so the
first run will source but not enrich until you add one.

**Dialer** is the calling desk. It needs your Twilio account; once your numbers
are synced, every lead gets a local caller ID. I build campaigns and the card
you see before each call. I never place a call.

**Newsletter** is for the ones who said "not now". Audiences are double
opt-in; connect Resend and verify your sending domain before the first issue.

**Desk** is the approval queue. Every email I draft and every talk track lands
there first. Nothing I write goes out until you approve it.

Where would you like to start?

- **Set up my pipeline** (rename the stages, add your custom fields)
- **Source my first list** (tell me your ICP and I will run it now)
- **Replace the sample data** (import your CSV or connect your CRM export)
